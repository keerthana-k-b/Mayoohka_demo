The Studio Story

Founded in Cherthala, Kerala by Celebrity Costume Designer Aiswarya Baiju, MAYOOKHA – The Bridal Studio is a premier bridal atelier and luxury destination dedicated to bespoke wedding couture, heirloom South Indian weaves, and master craftsmanship.

Specializing in pure Kanchipuram bridal silks, artisanal Kerala Kasavu drapes, intricately embellished bridal blouses, bespoke reception gowns, and celebratory family ensembles, every garment is thoughtfully conceptualized through one-on-one design consultations. We unite authentic heritage textiles with traditional adda frame zardozi, hand aari work, and flawless made-to-measure tailoring.

From styling acclaimed cinema personalities and high-profile wedding parties to creating cherished bridal trousseaus for brides across the world, MAYOOKHA turns your bridal dreams into timeless heirlooms.

Artisanal Dedication

Artisanal Craftsmanship

Refined techniques, hand-needle artistry, and master tailoring that define MAYOOKHA bridal creations.

Intricate Cutwork & Pearls

Architectural cutwork borders enriched with lustrous Swarovski seed pearls and fine metallic kasab wire on pure silk organza.

Bullion Wire Zardozi

Dimensional metal bullion wires and authentic zardozi needlework worked onto rich silk velvet and raw silk for royal ceremonial stature.

Adda Frame Artistry

Heritage wooden adda frames where master craftsmen dedicate countless hours to hand-stitching motifs, dabka, and tilla embroideries.

Bespoke Couture Design

Personalized silhouette rendering, garment draping, and customized neckline exploration guided by our head stylist.

How We Work

The 4-Step Bespoke Process

A seamless journey from initial inspiration to your cherished personal wardrobe.

01

Consult

A private one-on-one styling consultation in our store or over WhatsApp to explore your style preferences, color palette, and bespoke requests.

02

Design

Custom sketch conceptualization, authentic handloom fabric swatching, and precise neckline, sleeve, and drape tailoring specifications.

03

Handwork

Our artisans meticulously hand-embroider your garment on wooden adda frames with genuine metallic zardozi, seed pearls, and structured couture tailoring.

04

Delivery

Progressive fitting trials, final bespoke adjustments, and protective garment packaging delivered with care.
